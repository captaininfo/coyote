Put your online data to use. Locally.

Coyote turns your browsing + annotations into a private knowledge graph you control. A dynamic PKM, built for personal data‑driven analysis and to be a 100% private backend for pairing with local LLMs and/or AI agents.

How it works

Three simple steps to turn everyday browsing into a rich, machine‑readable personal knowledge graph.

1) Capture

Log searches, pages, and annotations automatically via the Coyote browser extension and Hypothes.is integration — with more platforms planned. You stay in control; capture is transparent and local.

2) Understand

Coyote builds a detailed, time‑stamped semantic record structured with Wikidata‑aligned entities and relationships. The PKG lives on your device, backed by open‑source Neo4j. Coyote never phones home.

3) Research & Model

Query, visualize, and analyze your PKG to model interests and lines of inquiry — or use it as a private backend for local LLMs and agent workflows (GraphRAG, RAG, and more).

The urgency of privacy in an AI age

AI thrives on personal context — but your context shouldn’t leak. Coyote’s default is local‑first and user‑controlled, so experimentation doesn’t trade away your data rights.

Local by design

Your data is processed and stored on your machine. No accounts required. No telemetry. No surprise cloud bills.

Transparent & inspectable

Open‑source code and a graph you can inspect. Trust comes from clarity — not black boxes.

Composable private AI

Pair your PKG with local LLMs/agents for private assistants that actually know you — without sending data away.
