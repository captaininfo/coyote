Put your online data to use. Locally.

Coyote turns your browsing + annotations into a private knowledge graph you control. A dynamic PKM, built for personal data‑driven analysis and to be a 100% private backend for pairing with local LLMs and/or AI agents.

How it works

Three simple steps to turn everyday browsing into a rich, machine‑readable personal knowledge graph.

1) Capture

Log searches, pages, and annotations automatically via the Coyote browser extension and Hypothes.is integration — with more platforms planned. You stay in control; capture is transparent and local.

2) Understand

Coyote builds a detailed, time‑stamped semantic record structured with Wikidata‑aligned entities and relationships. The PKG lives on your device, backed by open‑source Neo4j. Coyote never phones home.

3) Research & Model

Query, visualize, and analyze your PKG to model interests and lines of inquiry — or use it as a private backend for local LLMs and agent workflows (GraphRAG, RAG, and more).

The urgency of privacy in the age of AI

You’ll want what AI can do for you—without trading yourself to get it. Coyote’s default is local-first and user-controlled, so capability doesn’t require surrendering your context.

  1. AI’s upside is real. You will want the leverage and acceleration it provides.
  2. Power flows from personal data. The more context you give an AI, the better it gets—about you.
  3. Ubiquity is coming. AI will be woven into everyday tools and decisions.
  4. The extraction will be deep. The scope and intimacy of data harvested about your life and mind will be unprecedented.
  5. History warns us. Given past incentives and behavior, we should not expect external platforms to steward this power solely in your interest.

Use cases

Education PKM Quantified Self LLM RAG Open Recognition Learning Analytics

Education

Coyote turns students’ open-web reading and Hypothes.is annotations into a private, local knowledge graph for reflective writing, metacognition, and open-pedagogy—without sending data to third parties.

PKM

Capture your web research and annotations, enrich with topics/entities, and surface insights in your PKM. Search by semantic “aboutness,” not folders.

Quantified Self

A private, local “Fitbit for your mind”—spot patterns in what you read and how they relate to your goals and mood, without platform tracking.

LLM RAG

Pair Coyote’s local graph with your local LLM (e.g., Ollama) to run private RAG and agent workflows—no cloud required.

Open Recognition

Turn incidental learning into evidence: timestamped events, sources, topics/entities, and summaries you own and can audit—ready to support badges and micro-credentials.

Learning Analytics

Personal learning analytics without an LMS—event streams, topics/entities, and queryable graphs on your machine for PD and research. Ethics-first and IRB-friendly by design.
