Connect to Neo4j

Before Coyote can record your data, you must create a new Neo4j database, which includes creating a username and password.

 

 

Connect to Hypothes.is (Optional)

Hypothes.is is a social web annotation tool. Connecting to a Hypothes.is account is optional, but the data it generates will create a clearer picture of your learning and information behavior.

Step 1: Log in to Hypothes.is to get your username and API authentication token.

Log in to Hypothes.is. The login page will open in a new browser tab. Your Hypothes.is username is displayed at the top right corner of the Hypothes.is website after you log in. It's also the part of your profile URL that comes after https://hypothes.is/users/. After logging in to Hypothes.is and getting your username, return to this page.

Get your Hypothes.is API token. The Hypothes.is Developer page will open in a new tab. There, you'll find your personal API token. Highlight and copy the token.

Step 2: Enter Your Hypothes.is Username and API Token

 

Fetch Your Hypothes.is Data

Now that your account is connected, you can now use Hypothes.is to annotate web pages as you browse. Coyote will integrate your annotations into its analysis, enhancing your research by connecting your notes, questions, and highlights across different sources.

To pull your annotations into Coyote, you'll need to click the button below to fetch the data. Note that if you have an existing Hypothes.is account with existing data, the first time you fetch your data may take a significant amount of time.
